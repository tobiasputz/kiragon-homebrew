# Abyss — GM Running Sheet

**Level 13, Pathfinder 2e.** Planes of pure emotion. Manifestations are not demons and cannot die in the Abyss. The separate will-o'-wisp keeps its established identity. Keep original Kiragon Erosion stopwatch running (1 Identity/Will per bell).

|---|---|---|---|

| Grief | Comfort 3 VP | Diplomacy, Medicine, Religion, Occultism 31. CS 2 / S 1 / CF -1 | Grief comes with party |

## NPC quick numbers
- Joy L12: AC 33, HP 165, Fort 19 Ref 25 Will 22, Per 22.
- Anger L12: AC 33, HP 165, Fort 25 Ref 22 Will 19, Per 22.
- Resignation L16: AC 38, HP 295, Fort 28 Ref 25 Will 30, Per 28. Will DC 35 emanation; melee +30, frightened; 1d4 recharge. 60 damage in round briefly suppresses reaction, grants +2 to next rescue check.

## Permanent gifts (once/day)
- **Gift of Joy** [one action]: You + 1 willing ally within 30 feet gain 15 temp HP for 1 minute, reduce frightened by 1, and gain +1 status to Will saves vs emotion for 1 minute.
- **Gift of Anger** [reaction]: When you or an ally within 30 feet takes enemy damage, reduce it by 15; your next successful Strike before end of next turn deals +2d6 spirit.

## VP stage prompts for boss
1. **Failure:** “Tell me how you can promise he won't fail again.” Answer: You cannot promise success.
2. **Pain:** “If he had never wanted anything, he would never have suffered.” Answer: Pain doesn't prove caring was a mistake.
3. **Trying:** “What if he fails again?” Answer: Hope chooses to try anyway.

### End lines
